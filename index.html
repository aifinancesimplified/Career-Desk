The course

The CA course in four stages

The stages run in this order. The lead times below are the ones the checker and planner use to work out your registration dates.

  1. Foundation

    The first exam. Register after Class 10.

    Entry
    Register after Class 10. The first attempt comes after you appear in the Class 12 exam.ICAI FAQ
    Lead time
    Register at least 4 months before the exam month.ICAI FAQ
    Sittings
    January, May and September.ICAI FAQ
    Pass and validity
    40% in each paper and 50% overall, in one sitting. Registration lasts 4 years from the first eligible attempt.ICAI FAQ

  2. Intermediate

    Two groups, each assessed on its own or together.

    Entry
    Foundation passed and Class 12 done. Graduates enter directly with 55% in a commerce degree or 60% in any other degree.ICAI FAQ
    Lead time
    Register at least 8 months before the exam month.ICAI FAQ
    Sittings
    January, May and September.ICAI FAQ
    Pass and validity
    40% in each paper and 50% overall. Registration lasts 5 years and can be revalidated once for ₹400.ICAI FAQ

  3. Practical training

    Two years with a practising Chartered Accountant.

    Entry
    Both Intermediate groups passed, plus the four-week ICITSS course.ICAI FAQ
    Length
    2 years. Nine to twelve months of industrial training can sit inside it.ICAI FAQ
    Before Final
    Advanced ICITSS, completed after training and before the Final exam.ICAI FAQ

  4. Final

    The last exam. Then you apply for ICAI membership.

    Registration
    Any time after both Intermediate groups. The self-paced online modules are registered with it.ICAI FAQ
    To sit the exam
    Training finished 6 months before the exam month, Advanced ICITSS done, all four modules qualified.ICAI FAQ
    Sittings
    May and November.icai.org
    Validity
    10 years. Revalidation costs ₹500.ICAI FAQ

From Class 12, the fastest path works out to about 4.5 years. That is our estimate from ICAI's rules, not an ICAI figure, and every paper that does not clear adds time. Every blue tag on this page opens the ICAI page the fact came from.

Questions

Questions parents ask

Does my child need to be a topper?

ICAI's registration rules for the Foundation route list no minimum Class 10 or Class 12 percentage. What counts is clearing each exam: 40% in every paper and 50% overall, in a single sitting.ICAI FAQ

Can a Class 12 student go straight to Intermediate?

No. After Class 12 the route starts with Foundation. Direct Entry to Intermediate is for graduates and postgraduates with 55% in commerce or 60% in other streams, and for those who passed Intermediate at ICSI or ICMAI.ICAI FAQ

How long is practical training?

Two years, with nine to twelve months of industrial training allowed inside that time. It starts after both Intermediate groups and a four-week ICITSS course.ICAI FAQ

How many chances do students get?

Foundation registration lasts 4 years from the first eligible attempt and cannot be revalidated. Intermediate lasts 5 years and can be revalidated once for ₹400. Final registration lasts 10 years and can be revalidated for ₹500.ICAI FAQ

Can a student study for a degree alongside CA?

Many students pursue a degree while preparing for Foundation and Intermediate. Practical training takes most of the working week, so the degree mode needs planning before training starts.

How often is CA Final held now?

Twice a year, in May and November. News reports put ICAI's notification at 6 April 2026. Foundation and Intermediate stay at three sittings a year.icai.org
